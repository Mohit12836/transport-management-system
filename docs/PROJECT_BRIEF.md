# PROJECT BRIEF: NextGen Transport Management System (TMS Pro)

## 1. Executive Summary
The NextGen Transport Management System (TMS Pro) is a high-performance, multi-tenant cloud software designed for logistics operators, fleet owners, transport contractors, and freight brokers. The system unifies consignment booking (LR/Bilty), multi-stop dispatch, live vehicle tracking, fuel & FASTag accounting, driver advance & payroll settlements, vendor commission ledgers, and GST-compliant invoicing into a single real-time platform.

## 2. Business Problem & Opportunity
Transport and logistics businesses in India and emerging markets face severe operational bottlenecks:
- **Paper-Heavy LR Management:** Manual creation of Lorry Receipts (Consignment Notes) leads to lost records, billing delays, and dispute risks.
- **Uncontrolled Fleet Expenses:** Lack of real-time tracking for diesel slips, toll/FASTag charges, driver trip advances, and en-route repair expenses drains 8–15% of gross margins.
- **Disconnected Field Ops:** Drivers lack simple mobile interfaces for digital Proof of Delivery (POD) uploads, expense submission, and trip status updates.
- **Delayed Billing & Cash Flow Drag:** Invoicing takes weeks post-delivery due to slow POD collection and manual rate/tax computations.

## 3. Product Vision & Goals
- **100% Digital Workflow:** From booking creation -> LR generation -> trip dispatch -> en-route tracking -> POD capture -> automated invoicing & vendor settlement.
- **Strict Role-Based Multi-Tenancy:** Isolated environments for Super Admin, Organization Owner, Dispatch Manager, Accountant, Driver, and Third-Party Vendor/Broker.
- **Zero Financial Leakage:** Enforced trip expense approvals, vehicle-wise and trip-wise P&L calculations, automated driver advance deductions, and double-entry reconciliation.
- **Superior Modern Experience:** 100% responsive (Mobile 320px to 4K displays), tactile spring animations, fast keyboard data entry, and instant PDF/Excel exports.

## 4. Key Stakeholders & Personas
| Role | Key Focus | Primary Workflows |
|---|---|---|
| **Super Admin** | Platform SaaS health, tenant provisioning, system audit | Manage organizations, subscription plans, global settings, platform health |
| **Owner / MD** | High-level profitability, cash flow, fleet health | KPI dashboards, trip-wise P&L, balance sheets, compliance expiry alerts |
| **Dispatch / Branch Manager** | Daily dispatch, truck assignment, booking | Create bookings, generate LR, assign vehicles & drivers, track live trips, handle breakdowns |
| **Accountant** | Invoicing, payments, tax, advances | GST invoicing, customer receipts, fuel bills, driver advances/settlements, vendor bills |
| **Driver (Mobile-First)** | Simple trip task sheet, advance ledger, POD | View assigned trips, start/finish trip, submit diesel/toll slips, upload POD photo |
| **Vendor / Broker** | Vehicle placement, broker commission, billing | View attached vehicle trips, submit freight bills, track TDS and settlement status |
